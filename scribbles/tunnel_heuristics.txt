PREAMBLE
- *** all distance is measured relative to plate ****
- Metrics:
    - release_pos_x,release_pos_y,release_pos_z (directional release positions)
    - vx0,vy0,vz0 (initial directional velocities)
    - ax, ay, az (directional accelerations)
    - plate_x,plate_z (directional ball positions at plate)
- Constants:
    - plate_y = 0ft
    - tunnel_y = 23.8ft (https://www.baseballprospectus.com/news/article/31030/prospectus-feature-introducing-pitch-tunnels/)
        - decision-point based on average-velocity fastball; imposed as the general decision-point for all pitches as is the fastball-centric nature 
        of tunneling (i.e., conventionally speaking, you must be ready for the fastball, and so, make a swing decision by its decision point)
    - rubber_y = 60.5ft
    - ball_diam = 0.242ft
- Formulas:
    - ∆d = df - d0 [ft]
    - v = ∆d / ∆t [ft/s]
    - a = ∆v / ∆t [ft/s^2]

CRITERIA
    - two pitches tunnel w/ eachother such that they are within a ball of eachother at the tunneling point, i.e.,
        '''
        for a given pair of pitches (a, b):
            a & b are a tunnel pair if:
                |a_tunnel_xz(23.8ft) - b_tunnel_xz(23.8ft)| <= 0.242ft
        '''
